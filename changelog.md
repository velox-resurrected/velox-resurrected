### Velox Resurrected Main (0.19.3b) - Tinkers Release

Hope everyone has been having a good new year so far. With this update, I am happy to have Tinkers' Construct last seen in Velox Classic. This mod is fresh off the block, so if you experience any bugs related to it, make sure to report it to the mod author. With that said, I have worked hard to ensure the recyclability of the Create Crusher is carried over to Tinkers' Construct, so you can now melt hundreds of items/blocks into various molten metals. Also embers, and Tinkers' Construct work better now, outputting the same molten metal, and you can also alloy Dawnstone with Tinkers' forge. There is also a brand new questline for Tinkers' Construct, as well as other questlines such as Mana and Artifice, have been expanded upon.
Thank you, everyone, for your continued support, and for sharing, downloading, and playing Velox Resurrected. ♥

## Added Mods/Datapacks:

####Tinker's Construct
Tinkers' Construct is a mod about putting tools together in a wide variety of ways, then modifying them until they turn into something else. The tools never disappear and can be named and changed to your heart's desire. Once you make them, they're yours forever. Many different materials can be used to make your tools.
